About

The Photographer

Luz Hernandez Kroll

Wildlife & Nature Photographer

Luz Hernandez Kroll is a wildlife photographer based in Utah whose work captures the beauty, emotion, and quiet strength of animals in their natural world. Through patience and deep respect for wildlife, her images aim to reveal the personality and spirit of each creature she encounters — from the curious gaze of a fox to the watchful nature of birds and the rugged resilience of mountain animals.

Her photography has been featured in several publications. She was honored as a featured photographer in Everything Fox Magazine, where one of her images appeared on the cover and her work was showcased in an extensive 18-page editorial feature including her biography and fox photography. Her images were also included in wildlife stories about Rocky Mountain goats and the Onaqui wild horses in Utah's West Desert, documenting the experience of observing these remarkable animals in their natural habitats.

Luz and her husband were also selected as featured photographers in the international online publication The Universe of Colour Photography, where their work was highlighted in a multi-page editorial feature and interview.

Using a Canon EOS R5 and a Canon telephoto zoom lens, Luz spends countless hours in nature waiting for those fleeting moments when light, behavior, and emotion align. Her goal is not only to document wildlife, but to tell the quiet stories unfolding in the natural world.

Through her photography, Luz hopes to inspire a deeper appreciation for wildlife and the wild places they call home.

Issue 5, 2023

  • Cover Photograph
  • 18-Page Editorial Feature & Biography

Everything Fox Magazine

Spring 2024
  • Featured Photographers Randy Kroll and Luz Hernandez Kroll
  • Everything Wild Spotlight: Roaming Wonders
  • "Exploring Utah's Majestic Wild Horses"

This article highlighted the beauty and spirit of the wild horses of Utah's West Desert through the combined photography of Randy Kroll and Luz Hernandez Kroll. The feature included our biographies along with a collection of photographs capturing the power, grace, and freedom of these iconic animals in their natural landscape.

Everything Fox Magazine

Issue 6, 2025
  • Featured Photographer
  • Everything Wild Spotlight: Wildlife Wonders
  • "Discover the Rocky Mountain Goats of Mt. Timpanogos"

An article and photo essay documenting my experience photographing Rocky Mountain goats in the rugged terrain of Mt. Timpanogos.

"Witnessing mountain goats in their natural habitat is a humbling experience that serves as a reminder of the awe-inspiring beauty and power of nature. Their ability to thrive in some of the most inhospitable landscapes on Earth is a testament to their remarkable resilience and adaptability." — Luz

The Universe of Colour Photography

Online International Photography Magazine — Issue 1, Second Year, Autumn 2022
  • Featured Photographers Randy Kroll and Luz Hernandez Kroll
  • Cover Photograph — Randy Kroll
  • 39-Page Editorial Feature, Biographies & Double Interview

Both Randy Kroll and Luz Hernandez Kroll were featured in an extensive 39-page editorial spread in this international photography magazine. The feature included our biographies, a double interview discussing our photographic journeys and creative philosophies, and a curated collection of wildlife and landscape photographs from both photographers. The cover image for the issue was a portrait of Luz captured by Randy Kroll.
